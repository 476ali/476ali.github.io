The process of healing is not to return to a prior state; it is a process
forward, one that ramifies with respect to how, when, and where inflicted. This
is the theory of *Un Cœur Blessé* that the fact of hurt is but simply an aspect
of change.

See the phoenix. How it bursts into flames and burns away to ashes and how
likewise it begins again from the same and rises anew. See how it thus becomes
pliable and therefore perpetual.
